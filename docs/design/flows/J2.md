## Exceptions

| Exception ID | PRD §8 term | Trigger | Screens | Outcome | Status term |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| J2-EX-01 | expired intent | The QR code or payment request has expired | CON-036, CON-037 | Nothing is charged; the payer is asked to request a new QR | ST-EXPIRED |
| J2-EX-02 | duplicate capture | The same payment is captured twice | CON-037, MER-007 | One payment only; the second capture is ignored and explained | ST-COMPLETED |
| J2-EX-03 | disputed transaction | The payer disputes a payment | MER-007, CON-041 | The payment shows under review with the dispute reason | ST-UNDER-REVIEW |
| J2-EX-04 | partial settlement failure | Some items in a settlement batch fail | MER-013 | Failed items show with Retry; the others stay settled | ST-FAILED |
