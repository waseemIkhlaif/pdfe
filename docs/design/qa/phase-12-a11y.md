# Phase 12 accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Method: Automated scan on every screen plus a manual screen-reader pass on iOS VoiceOver, Android TalkBack and one desktop screen reader, against docs/design/system/accessibility-rules.md (A-1 to A-12).

Every finding gets a resolution status. Findings of severity `serious` or `critical` cannot be `accepted` (check D-1). The audit covers all 8 interfaces plus admin: CON (mobile and web), MER, BIZ, OPS, CMP, FIN, INV, DEV.
